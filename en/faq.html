FAQ

Questions before you buy

Products, quality, safety, shipping and returns. Can't find your answer? Email service@uhealthfit.com.

Products & use

Are these medicines? Can they replace what my doctor prescribed?

No. The capsules are dietary supplements and the eye patches, creams and serum are cosmetics. None of them is a medicine, and none replaces a diagnosis, treatment or prescription. If you take medication, are pregnant or nursing, or have a health concern, talk to your doctor before use.

How long until I notice anything?

We don't promise timelines. Herbal formulas are designed for steady daily use, and individual experiences vary.

How do I take the capsules?

Most are 3 capsules twice a day with meals (Calmoon: with lunch and with dinner). Each bottle holds 180 capsules. Every product page has exact directions.

Can I combine products from different collections?

Many people do — the eye patches and LumiView, for example. If you take several supplements or any prescription medication, check with your doctor or pharmacist first.

Do the eye patches go in my eyes?

No. They sit on the skin at the inner corner, outer corner and below each eye. The cooling ingredients can make some people's eyes water a little; if you feel stinging or irritation, remove the patches and rinse with water. Patch test first; not for anyone under 18, pregnant or nursing, taking blood thinners or planning eye surgery within 2 weeks.

Are the capsules vegan?

The capsule shells are plant-based (HPMC). Check each product's ingredient list for details.

Quality & safety

Where are the products made?

The eye patches are made in Japan. Capsules, creams and the serum are made in the USA with imported botanical ingredients, in cGMP facilities, and finished products are third-party tested for purity.

What does “FDA-registered facility” mean?

It means the manufacturing facility is registered with the FDA. That isn't FDA approval — dietary supplements and cosmetics are not approved by the FDA before sale.

What about contaminants in Chinese herbs?

It's a fair question for any imported botanical. Our finished products are third-party tested for purity. For product-specific questions, email service@uhealthfit.com.

Could these interact with my medication?

Possibly. Several botanicals, including salvia, dong quai and schisandra, can interact with blood thinners such as warfarin, and Calmoon shouldn't be combined with sedatives without medical advice. Bring the label to your doctor or pharmacist.
