FAQ

About Teeth & Gums

Does this replace seeing a dentist?

No. Dentaforte is a dietary supplement, not a treatment. Keep your cleanings, exams and anything your dentist recommends; think of it as nutritional support that runs in parallel.

How long until I notice anything?

We can't promise a timeline. The formula is designed for steady daily use over months rather than a short course, and individual experiences vary.

I take a blood thinner. Can I use it?

Talk to your doctor first. Several botanicals in the formula, including salvia and schisandra, can interact with anticoagulants.

Is it easy for an older parent to take?

It's six capsules a day, three with breakfast and three with dinner — keeping the bottle next to the breakfast table is the easiest reminder. If your parent takes medication, check with their doctor or pharmacist first.
