FAQ

About Eye Health

How are cooling patches different from a warm eye mask?

A warm or steam mask relaxes with heat for a short session. Our patches are cooling hydrogel you can wear for hours, overnight if you like, with botanical extracts on the skin around the eyes. Different feel, different moment — some people use both.

I already take an eye vitamin. Can I add these?

The patches and eye cream are topical, so they don't overlap with a supplement. LumiView is a different approach from a lutein or zeaxanthin supplement; if you'd like to take both, check with your doctor or pharmacist first.

Do the patches go in the eye?

No. They sit on the skin at the inner corner, outer corner and below the eye — never on the eye itself. They're made in Japan and preservative-free; do a 24-hour patch test before first use.

Do I still need eye exams?

Yes. Nothing here replaces glasses, contacts, prescribed eye drops or regular eye exams.
