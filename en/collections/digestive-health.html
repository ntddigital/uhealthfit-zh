FAQ

About Digestive Comfort

Can I take it with probiotics or fiber?

They take different approaches, so they don't overlap. If you take several supplements or any medication, check with your doctor or pharmacist about combining them.

Can I take it every day?

Yes — HerbalFlow is designed for steady daily use with meals and contains no senna or stimulant herbs. Follow the suggested use.

When should I see a doctor instead?

If you have ongoing abdominal pain, blood in the stool, unexplained weight change or a sudden change in your habits, see a doctor rather than relying on a supplement.
