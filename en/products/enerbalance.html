FAQ

Before you buy

Will it keep me up like coffee?

EnerBalance contains no caffeine and no stimulants. It takes a nourishing approach, so take it with meals and see how it fits your day.

What is a herbal paste (gao)?

A traditional preparation: botanicals simmered together, strained and reduced to a concentrate. EnerBalance keeps that kind of concentrate and seals it into capsules.

American or Asian ginseng — why American?

American ginseng is traditionally considered the gentler, “cooler” ginseng, suited to everyday use. EnerBalance pairs it with warming astragalus for balance.

How much do I take?

3 capsules twice a day with food — 6 capsules, 3,000 mg. One bottle holds 60 servings.

Who is it for?

People working long days, anyone cutting back on caffeine, older adults who want gentle everyday support, and people training without leaning on stimulants.†

Who shouldn't take it?

It's not for pregnant or nursing women. If you're under 18, take blood thinners or other medication, or have a medical condition, talk to your doctor first. Don't use if the safety seal is broken.
