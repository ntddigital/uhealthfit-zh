FAQ

Before you buy

Is it normal for my eyes to water a little?

The cooling ingredients can make some people's eyes water a little. If you feel stinging or irritation, remove the patches and rinse with water.

Why do you suggest bedtime?

It's the easiest time to give the patches their full wear time: they become part of your wind-down and come off in the morning. Daytime use works too, as a shorter cooling break.

How is this different from a warm eye mask?

A warm or steam mask relaxes with heat for a short session. These are cooling hydrogel patches you can wear for hours, with botanical extracts on the skin. Some people use both, at different times.

Can I use them with eye drops?

Yes — apply the patches about 30 minutes after drops so the drops absorb first. If you use prescription eye medication, check with your eye doctor.

Can contact-lens wearers use them?

Yes. Take your lenses out first.

Is there an allergy risk?

The formula is preservative-free and made to be gentle, but any botanical can irritate some people. Do a 24-hour patch test on the inner forearm before first use, especially if your skin tends to react.
