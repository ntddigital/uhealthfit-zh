FAQ

Before you buy

Does this replace seeing a dentist?

No. Dentaforte is a dietary supplement, not a treatment. Keep your cleanings, exams and anything your dentist recommends.

How long until I notice anything?

We can't promise a timeline. The formula is designed for steady daily use over months rather than a short course, and we'd rather be honest about that than overpromise.

Can I take it if I'm on blood thinners?

Several botanicals in the formula, including salvia and schisandra, have known interactions with anticoagulants. Please talk to your doctor before starting.

Why six capsules a day instead of one?

A 3,000 mg daily blend won't fit in one capsule you'd want to swallow. Splitting it into two servings also spreads it across the day.

What if I miss a day or a week?

Don't try to catch up by doubling the dose — just restart the normal routine. Consistency over months matters more than any single day.

Where are the herbs grown?

They're sourced from licensed growers across East Asia, where these plants are native, and encapsulated in a cGMP-registered facility in the US.
