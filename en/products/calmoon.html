FAQ

Before you buy

Why do I take it at midday?

Because the formula looks after the day's tension, not just the last hour. The midday dose eases the morning's stress before it builds; the evening dose supports a natural wind-down.†

No melatonin — so how does it work?

It relies on a traditional multi-herb formula that supports the body's own rhythm, rather than supplying a sleep hormone.†

Will I feel groggy in the morning?

Calmoon is formulated for a clear-headed wake-up and contains no hormones. Everyone responds differently, so start with the suggested use and notice how you feel. Don't drive or operate machinery after taking it.

How much do I take each day?

6 capsules (3,000 mg): 3 with lunch and 3 with dinner.

Why seven herbs?

Classical formulas give each herb a role. Jujube seed, poria and lily bulb for calm; albizia and self-heal for mood and tension; eleuthero for everyday stress; salvia for the mind and circulation.†

Can I take it with other medication?

If you take blood thinners such as warfarin, sedatives, sleep medication or any other medication, or have a health condition, talk to your doctor first. Not for use during pregnancy or while nursing.
