FAQ

Before you buy

Which skin types is it for?

Oily skin that's shiny soon after washing, and combination skin with an oily T-zone. Because it's acid-free and gentle, it also suits skin that reacts to stronger products — patch test first.

Will it make my skin feel tight?

It's designed to balance the look of shine without the stripped feeling of harsh cleansers. If an area feels dry, use it only where you're shiny and the Soothing & Repair Cream elsewhere.

Can I use it with retinol or acids?

Space them out and watch how your skin feels; patch test when introducing anything new.

Do I need sunscreen?

Yes — daily sunscreen is the foundation of any face routine.

How long does a jar last?

About 60 days, morning and night.
