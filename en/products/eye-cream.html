FAQ

Before you buy

When and how much?

Nightly on clean skin, and during the day when you need it. A rice-sized amount per eye, patted along the orbital bone, avoiding the eyelid and waterline.

Does it replace the patches?

It's the daytime relay, not a replacement. When you have time at night, the patches give the full cooling ritual.

Can men use it?

Yes.

Is it okay for sensitive skin?

It's made to be gentle, but do a patch test first — especially if eucalyptus or essential oils tend to bother your skin.

Will it sting?

Eucalyptus gives a cool feel. Keep the cream on the skin around the eyes, not in them; if it gets into the eye, rinse with water.

How long does a jar last?

About 60 days of nightly use.
